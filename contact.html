Frequently Asked Questions

We offer nationwide delivery across Pakistan in 3 to 5 working days via insured courier partners. Orders of Rs. 10,000 or above qualify for free shipping, while orders below Rs. 10,000 carry a flat nationwide shipping fee of Rs. 250.

We offer a hassle-free 14-day easy exchange policy across Pakistan. If you wish to exchange your piece for another hue or silhouette, simply contact our WhatsApp Concierge (+92 346 9377256) with your order reference and we will arrange a doorstep exchange for any unused item in its original dust bag and gift box.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available nationwide as our default payment method. We also accept Bank Transfer, JazzCash, and Easypaisa — once your order is placed, our concierge team contacts you on WhatsApp or phone to confirm your order and share account details.

Our full-grain leather is treated with a breathable protective finish. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after wear, store upright inside the provided blush linen dust bag with tissue stuffing away from humidity, and condition twice a year with neutral leather balm.
